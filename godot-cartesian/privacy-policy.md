## 隐私政策

《Godot Cartesian》完全离线运行，并且不收集、存储或上传任何个人信息。

- 不包含任何网络请求、广告 SDK、分析工具或第三方追踪组件
- 不要求注册账号或登录
- 关卡进度和最高分仅保存在本机的应用沙盒目录
- 不需要网络、摄像头、麦克风、定位或通讯录权限

因此，本游戏在 App Store 中可按“无数据收集”原则提交审核。

## Privacy Policy

Godot Cartesian runs fully offline and does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

- No network requests, ad SDKs, analytics, or third-party tracking
- No account registration or sign-in
- Save data for progress and best scores is kept only on the local device sandbox
- No network, camera, microphone, location, or contacts permission is required

This game is suitable for App Store submission under a No Data Collected privacy label.
